About the company

A simple way to shop abroad

We're a logistics company that helps you order goods from the USA and Europe and receive them in Kazakhstan quickly and without hassle.

You find the item you want on any foreign website — we buy it, bring it to our warehouse, process it and deliver it straight to you. We work with clothing, footwear, electronics, accessories and other categories of goods.

Our goal is to make international shopping simple and accessible for everyone, without complicated schemes or middlemen with unclear terms.
